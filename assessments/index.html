Working Genius

I completed The Table Group's Working Genius Assessment, a model that identifies six types of genius required to accomplish any kind of work. Two are natural gifts that give me energy and joy (Working Genius), two are activities I can do reasonably well without being energized by them (Working Competency), and two drain my energy (Working Frustration). My pairing is ET | TE, The Loyal Finisher, combining Tenacity and Enablement. Hover, tap, or focus each gear below to explore the full model.

Working Genius Pairing, ET | TE
The Loyal Finisher, Tenacity and Enablement

I derive joy and energy from completing tasks and responding to the needs of others in the pursuit of getting things done. I am quick to answer the call to action, equally valuing responsiveness and reliability. I am a low-maintenance contributor who holds myself accountable for delivering on my promises, though I have a hard time saying no to requests and can find myself over-committed, prone to burnout and to feeling underappreciated.

Genius, my natural gifts Competency, capable but not energizing Frustration, drains my energy

How I See Myself

I see myself very much in the Loyal Finisher profile. This is how the six types of genius play out in my daily work.

Gives me energy

Tenacity & Enablement

  • Seeing things move from idea to done: a published paper, a delivered analysis, a working tool.
  • Being the person colleagues turn to, whether it is getting code to run, reviewing an analysis or sharing the load on a task.
  • Responding quickly and being someone others can count on.
I do it well, but it does not energize me

Invention & Galvanizing

  • Coming up with new solutions when a problem calls for it.
  • Rallying people and getting a team moving on a project.
Wears me out

Wonder & Discernment

  • Long abstract phases, speculating without a clear path forward.
  • Judging ideas on intuition alone, without data or criteria to lean on.

The same profile points to something I have been working on: saying no. Because I want to help and deliver on everything, I sometimes take on more than I should. I have been learning to prioritize better, agree on realistic deadlines and ask for clarity early on, so that my effort goes where it truly makes a difference.

Como Eu Me Vejo

Eu me reconheço muito no perfil de Loyal Finisher. É assim que os seis tipos de genialidade aparecem no meu dia a dia de trabalho.

Me dá energia

Tenacity & Enablement

  • Ver as coisas saírem do papel e chegarem ao fim: um artigo publicado, uma análise entregue, uma ferramenta funcionando.
  • Ser a pessoa a quem os colegas recorrem, seja para destravar um código, revisar uma análise ou dividir uma tarefa.
  • Responder rápido e ser alguém com quem os outros podem contar.
Faço bem, mas não me energiza

Invention & Galvanizing

  • Criar soluções novas quando o problema pede.
  • Mobilizar pessoas e colocar uma equipe em movimento num projeto.
Me desgasta

Wonder & Discernment

  • Fases muito abstratas, de ficar especulando sem um caminho claro.
  • Avaliar ideias só pela intuição, sem dados ou critérios em que me apoiar.

Esse mesmo perfil traz um ponto que venho trabalhando: a dificuldade de dizer não. Por querer ajudar e cumprir tudo, às vezes assumo mais do que deveria. Tenho aprendido a priorizar melhor, combinar prazos realistas e pedir clareza logo no início, para que meu esforço vá para onde realmente faz diferença.
