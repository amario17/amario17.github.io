João today.

João hoje.

I was born on December 17, 1999, in Ananás, in northern Tocantins. My mother raised me and my six siblings on her own, and I grew up working in the fields with my family.

At 13, I left home to study at the Federal Institute of Tocantins (IFTO), in Araguatins, where I completed a technical program in agricultural sciences and, later, my degree in Agronomic Engineering.

Today I am a Ph.D. candidate in Genetics and Plant Breeding at UFV. I work with quantitative genetics, genomic prediction, mixed models and environmental data to predict how genotypes perform in environments that were not tested.

Nasci em 17 de dezembro de 1999, em Ananás, no norte do Tocantins. Minha mãe criou sozinha a mim e aos meus seis irmãos, e cresci trabalhando na roça com a minha família.

Aos 13 anos, saí de casa para estudar no Instituto Federal do Tocantins (IFTO), em Araguatins, onde fiz o curso técnico em Agropecuária e, depois, a graduação em Engenharia Agronômica.

Hoje sou doutorando em Genética e Melhoramento na UFV. Trabalho com genética quantitativa, predição genômica, modelos mistos e dados ambientais para predizer o desempenho de genótipos em ambientes não avaliados.

2024 – present

Ph.D. Researcher, Genetics & Breeding

Federal University of Viçosa (UFV) / IDATA, QGSL Lab

Ph.D. in Genetics and Plant Breeding under Professor Kaio Olímpio. I work on genomic prediction, environmental covariates (enviromics) and mixed models to support genotype selection and cultivar recommendation.

Read full storyShow less

My research models genotype-by-environment interaction with mixed models and integrates environmental covariates into genomic prediction, to predict how genotypes will perform in environments that were not tested. I also lead a partnership project with GDM Seeds on continental-scale soybean data and develop qgtools, a set of R functions for these analyses.

During the Ph.D., I won the 3rd GDM Universities Challenge and the UFV–Bayer Hackathon, taught courses on enviromics, and took part in the development of sweet corn populations, from crosses between inbred lines to new base populations.

2022 – 2024

M.Sc. Researcher, Genetics & Breeding

Federal University of Viçosa (UFV)

After working in the field, I returned to academia for an M.Sc. in Genetics and Plant Breeding at UFV, working with segregating wheat populations.

Read full storyShow less

Each segregating generation showed a different level of genetic variability. I modeled the generation effect in joint analyses, so that populations evaluated in different generations could be analyzed together and selected more accurately. This work was published in Euphytica (2025).

The M.Sc. is where I moved from running field trials to developing analytical approaches, which led me to a Ph.D. focused on modeling, prediction and data integration.
