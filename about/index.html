João today.

João hoje.

I was born on December 17, 1999, in Ananás, in northern Tocantins, into a simple family raised on the land. My mother raised me and my six siblings on her own, and in that context, studying wasn't a common reality. It was working the fields that taught me, from an early age, about effort, responsibility, and above all, about the importance of seeking a different life through education.

At 13, I left home to study at the Federal Institute of Tocantins, in Araguatins. It was there that a new world of science, technology and possibilities opened up to me. It was also when I realized that knowledge could take me far beyond the place where I was born, doing what I loved: working with agriculture.

Looking back today, I see that decision at 13 completely changed the course of my life. I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Genetics and Plant Breeding, working with quantitative genetics, genomic prediction, machine learning, and data integration to develop new breeding strategies. I still work with what I knew as a child, agriculture, but now using science and technology to help transform it. For someone from a family where almost no one had the chance to reach higher education, science was never just a profession: it represents the possibility of transforming a reality. Every step I take today is a continuation of that decision I made at 13, to study in order to change my life and, perhaps, other people's lives too.

2022 – 2024

M.Sc. Researcher, Genetics & Breeding

Federal University of Viçosa (UFV)

After my professional experience in the field, I returned to academia to pursue my master's in Genetics and Breeding, working with segregating wheat populations. This period represented a new stage in my training, as I began to bring together my hands-on experience in breeding programs with a deeper understanding of quantitative genetics and data analysis.

Read full storyShow less

Working with different segregating generations, I realized that each generation showed different levels of genetic variability, and that this information needed to be properly accounted for in the analyses. Through statistical modeling, I incorporated the generation effect into joint models, making it possible to analyze different generations simultaneously and improve selection capacity within the breeding program.

It was during my master's that I began to see the enormous potential of data analysis as a tool to transform the efficiency of breeding programs. The combination of my hands-on experience in the field, following production systems and real agricultural challenges, with the knowledge I gained in quantitative genetics, showed me that I could contribute not just by running experiments, but also by developing and applying new analytical approaches. That realization was decisive in my choice to continue into a Ph.D. focused on modeling, prediction, and data integration, aiming to develop tools that help breeders make better decisions.

Mestrando em Genética e Melhoramento

Universidade Federal de Viçosa (UFV)

Após minha experiência profissional no campo, retornei à academia para realizar o mestrado em Genética e Melhoramento, trabalhando com populações segregantes de trigo. Esse período representou uma nova etapa da minha formação, pois comecei a unir minha experiência prática em programas de melhoramento com uma visão mais aprofundada de genética quantitativa e análise de dados.

Ver texto completoMostrar menos

Trabalhando com diferentes gerações segregantes, percebi que cada geração apresentava diferentes níveis de variabilidade genética e que essa informação precisava ser considerada de forma adequada nas análises. Por meio da modelagem estatística, incorporei o efeito de geração em modelos conjuntos, permitindo analisar diferentes gerações simultaneamente e melhorar a capacidade de seleção dentro do programa de melhoramento.

Foi durante o mestrado que comecei a perceber o enorme potencial da análise de dados como ferramenta para transformar a eficiência dos programas de melhoramento. A combinação entre minha vivência no campo, acompanhando sistemas produtivos e desafios reais da agricultura, e o conhecimento adquirido em genética quantitativa mostrou que eu poderia contribuir não apenas conduzindo experimentos, mas também desenvolvendo e aplicando novas abordagens analíticas. Essa percepção foi decisiva para minha escolha de seguir no doutorado em uma linha mais voltada à modelagem, predição e integração de dados, buscando desenvolver ferramentas que auxiliem os melhoristas na tomada de decisão.
